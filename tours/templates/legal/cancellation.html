Cancellation Policy

Please review our refund guidelines.

Deposit Policy

All deposits (typically 20% of the total trip cost) are strictly non-refundable. This secures your booking and covers initial operational costs.

Final Balance

The remaining balance must be paid by the due date. If the balance is not received by the due date, your booking may be cancelled, and the deposit will not be refunded.

How to Cancel

You can cancel your booking directly from your user dashboard by navigating to "My Profile" and clicking "Cancel Booking".
